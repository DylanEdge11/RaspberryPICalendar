Import from Google Photos

Search for your album by name, select up to 100 photos, then import them here. Display-sized copies stay on this Pi until you remove them. New album additions are not imported automatically. Videos are skipped.

Anyone paired with this household display can start an import. In Google Photos, sign in with the same account connected below. Only your selected photos are downloaded; Google passwords and tokens are never sent to this page.

First-time setup: enable Google Photos Picker API in Google Cloud, then open http://localhost:8080/control in Chromium on the Pi and connect below. After setup, use these controls from your phone.
